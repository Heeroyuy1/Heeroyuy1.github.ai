AMS — Asset Management System

A professional Windows desktop application for tracking physical assets across your organization. Generate barcodes, scan QR codes from your phone, import managed devices from Microsoft Entra ID, manage check-in / check-out workflows, run reports, and maintain a full audit trail — all from one app.

📦 Asset Tracking 📱 Barcode & QR 🔁 Check-In/Out 📊 Reports 🔑 Licensed Editions ☁️ Azure Import 📲 Mobile Scanner 📄 Excel / CSV / PDF Export
Overview

Know Exactly Where Every Asset Is

AMS replaces spreadsheets and sticky notes with a searchable database of your physical assets. Track laptops, tools, equipment, furniture, vehicles — anything with a serial number or tag. Generate barcodes for labeling, scan QR codes on mobile, and maintain a complete history of who had what and when.

📦

Full Asset CRUD

Create, update, and manage assets with custom fields, categories, purchase info, warranty dates, and attached files. Bulk import from Excel or CSV.

📱

Barcode & QR Codes

Generate printable barcodes and QR codes for every asset. Scan with any phone camera for instant lookup. Label everything in your inventory.

🔁

Check-In / Check-Out

Track who has which asset right now. Log check-outs to employees with due dates, notes, and status tracking. Never lose an asset again.

👥

People & Places

Manage employees, departments, and locations. Assign assets to people and places with full history. Code-based employee and department lookup.

📊

Reports & Dashboards

KPI cards, asset status charts, low-stock alerts, and recent activity. Export inventory reports, warranty tracking, and assignment summaries to Excel, CSV, or PDF.

🕐

Complete Audit Trail

Every asset has a full history log. Every action is recorded in audit logs. Know exactly who changed what and when — compliance-ready out of the box.

💾

Auto Backup & Restore

Scheduled automatic backups (default: every 24 hours, max 10 retained). Manual backup and restore from the Settings panel. SQLite WAL mode — safe for forced shutdowns.

🔐

Role-Based Access

Six roles: Admin, Manager, User, Technician, Auditor, Read Only. Admin-only user management in Settings — create users, assign roles, reset passwords, and control access with scrypt-hashed passwords and session tokens.

📥

Import from Anywhere

Excel (.xlsx/.xls) and CSV import with column mapping preview. Map your existing spreadsheet columns to AMS fields in a visual mapper before importing.

☁️

Azure Device Import

Import managed devices directly from Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD). Automatic lifecycle classification — active, inactive, stale, investigation required, or candidate removal. Encrypted credentials at rest with AES-256-GCM.

📲

Mobile Barcode Scanner

Companion mobile app pairs with AMS over your local network. Scan barcodes and QR codes with your phone camera for instant asset lookup — no dedicated scanner hardware needed.

Editions

Choose Your License

Three editions to fit your organization's size. License keys activate features instantly via the app's License panel. All data stays local — no cloud dependency.

Technical

Built for Reliability

🖥

Desktop-First

Windows native application built on Electron. No browser, no cloud dependency. Your data stays on your machine.

🗄

SQLite Database

Fast, reliable, zero-configuration database. WAL mode for concurrent reads. 64MB memory cache. 12 tables with full foreign key integrity.

🔒

Secure by Default

Context isolation enabled. No Node.js access from the renderer. Scrypt-hashed passwords. Session tokens with configurable timeout. All IPC calls validated.

📝

Audit Logging

Every create, update, delete, login, and export is logged with user ID, timestamp, and details. JSON structured logging with rotation.

Getting Started

Quick Setup

1

Download & Install

Download the installer or portable EXE. Run the NSIS installer or extract the zip. Launch Woven Model AMS from your desktop or Start Menu.

2

Create Admin Account

On first launch, the Setup page appears. Create your admin account with username and password. This will be the system administrator.

3

Set Up Your Organization

Add departments, locations, and employees. Or import them in bulk from a CSV or Excel spreadsheet using the Import tool.

4

Start Tracking Assets

Add your first assets — manually or import from a spreadsheet. Generate barcodes, assign to employees, and start the check-in/check-out workflow.
